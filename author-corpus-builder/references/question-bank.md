# Question bank

Use this bank to build a creator corpus. Ask all 50 questions for a full corpus. Ask 10 at a time when the user wants a gradual interview.

For every question, encourage concrete scenes, exact phrases, body sensations, and examples. The goal is not a neat biography. The goal is raw material.

## A. Identity and temperament

1. Without using a job title, what are the three most stable traits in you? When did each one first become obvious?
2. What part of you has stayed the same across different environments?
3. What kind of person do you hate being mistaken for? Why does that misunderstanding bother you?
4. What are several moments where you thought, "yes, this is exactly how I am"?
5. What have you been trying to escape for a long time?
6. What have you been chasing for a long time, even if the name has changed?
7. What kind of person do you envy? Focus on their state, not their result.
8. What kind of success do you instinctively distrust?
9. If you named the stages of your past ten years, what would they be called?
10. How is the person you want to become now different from the person you wanted to become five years ago?

## B. Lived experience

11. When did you first strongly feel, "I cannot keep living like this"?
12. What failure still feels expensive? Did it cost money, time, opportunity, dignity, or something else?
13. When did you work hard and later realize the direction was wrong?
14. What small choice ended up shaping you more than expected?
15. When did something suddenly click for you? What were you stuck on before that?
16. What have you delayed for a long time? Why did it stay delayed?
17. What do you say you do not care about, while your actions show that you do?
18. What period of life do you never want to repeat? Describe one ordinary day inside it.
19. When did you feel yourself becoming dull, numb, or consumed?
20. When did you feel that you were finally acting more like yourself?

## C. Work, tools, and AI

21. Why did AI first attract you: speed, capability, freedom, creativity, control, or something else?
22. What was the first task where AI felt genuinely useful?
23. When did AI feel annoying or unreliable?
24. What is your biggest expectation for AI now? What life expectation sits behind it?
25. What kind of tool experience hits your pain point the hardest?
26. What freedom has technology given you? What cage has it created?
27. How does your "programmer mindset" show up? How does it slow you down?
28. What technical project absorbed you while helping you avoid a more real problem?
29. What standards do you now use to judge whether a tool is worth using?
30. If you had to tell one story about AI implementation being less glamorous than people think, which one would you tell?

## D. Media and expression

31. Why do you want to make content? Give the honest reason, including the less respectable parts.
32. What scares you most when publishing: being ignored, misunderstood, exposed, unprofessional, or something else?
33. What side of yourself do you want people to see through your content? What side do you hide?
34. Which past post, comment, or reply felt most like you? Why?
35. Which post made you regret the way you expressed yourself or the amount you revealed?
36. What does "human-sounding content" mean to you? Give examples.
37. What content style makes you physically uncomfortable or annoyed?
38. What do you usually think right before pressing publish?
39. What are you qualified to talk about? What are you not ready to talk about?
40. If you posted for 100 days, where would the habit most likely break?

## E. Money, freedom, and markets

41. What is your real emotion toward making money: excitement, anxiety, shame, desire, suspicion, or a mix?
42. When did you first feel market feedback?
43. What payment, no matter how small, changed how you understood your ability?
44. How do you see the relationship between salary cash flow and doing your own thing?
45. Which ability is most likely to earn your first more-free money?
46. What are you least willing to sacrifice for money?
47. What real demand have you noticed but not acted on?
48. How do you judge whether a demand is real?
49. What is your biggest commercial weakness? How does it show up in ordinary life?
50. If you could validate only one business direction in the next year, what would you choose?

## Optional follow-up prompts

Use these when an answer is too abstract:

- Tell me the exact day or scene where you learned this.
- What did your body feel like in that moment?
- What sentence did you say to yourself at the time?
- What did you lose, and what did you gain?
- Who else was in the scene?
- What would you have been embarrassed to admit then?
- What do you believe now because of that moment?
